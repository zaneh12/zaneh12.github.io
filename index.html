A quick intro

I'm Zane. I work on forecasting, market structure, and risk systems. I like the point where a view becomes a model, the model becomes code, and the code has to survive messy data, changing assumptions, and real use.

My background is in statistics and economics, and the work I keep coming back to is financial modeling, market problems, and building tools that stay useful after the first pass. Right now that means fixed income hedging, MBS, and rate-driven scenarios where duration can change faster than the hedge.

Now

  • Risk and forecasting at Charles Schwab
  • Thinking about fixed income hedging and MBS
  • Keeping code and notes in public view
  • Building tools that need to survive rate moves
