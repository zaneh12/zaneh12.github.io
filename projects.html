Forecasting

Forecasting

Kairosis is a forecast aggregation method from my thesis. It estimates where a forecast stream changes, then weights the forecasts from the relevant part of the window more heavily.

The repo includes the implementation, tests, and evaluation scripts, so the method can be inspected and reproduced.
