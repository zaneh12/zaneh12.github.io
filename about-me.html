About

I'm Zane Hassoun.

I work in quantitative risk at Charles Schwab focusing on crypto currency and machine learning. Outside of work, the problems I enjoy most sit across crypto, credit, rates, FX, and quant development: building systems that price, hedge, and explain what changes when markets move. Before that I completed a PhD in statistics at the University of York. Across all of it, the focus has been on models, code, and financial systems that remain useful when the inputs are messy and the assumptions need to hold up in production.

Current role

Quantitative Risk in Digital Assets at Charles Schwab.

My work centers on understanding risk, model behavior, and failure modes in digital asset workflows. I work with both classical risk models and newer generative AI approaches, with an emphasis on keeping the models stable as market conditions and data sources change.
